Machine learning is one of the most important fields in modern computer science and artificial intelligence. It allows systems to learn from data, identify patterns, and make decisions with minimal human intervention. Engineers use machine learning algorithms in applications such as recommendation systems, speech recognition, image classification, and autonomous vehicles. Learning machine learning requires understanding mathematics, programming, and data processing techniques. With the rise of big data and cloud computing, machine learning has become a powerful tool that drives innovation across industries. Students who practice building real-world projects gain deeper insight into how models are trained, evaluated, and improved over time.
